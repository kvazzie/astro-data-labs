Astro content
under a SQL microscope

Load Astro's content store, project ordinary collections into one SQL table, and inspect them with DuckDB in this browser tab. Selected files stay local and their contents are never modified.

Experiment question Is the path .astro/data-store.json → devalue.parse() → rows → DuckDB useful for inspecting collections, field differences, and references? Direct access to Astro's generated store depends on its internal format. This Experiment makes no compatibility promise.
